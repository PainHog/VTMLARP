# Compendium Source Data

Each subfolder here corresponds to a compendium pack declared in `system.json`. Foundry v12 reads these folders directly as "source" compendiums — one JSON file per document, no build/compile step required.

- `attributes/` — the 27 named Physical/Social/Mental Traits (e.g. Brawny, Charismatic, Clever) plus their Negative-Trait counterparts, as Attribute items (92 files: 20 positive + 10/11/10 negative per category)
- `abilities/` — the 30 core Abilities (Talents/Skills/Knowledges) as Ability items
- `disciplines/` — Discipline and Power items (drag onto a character's Powers tab). Includes Sabbat-sourced content from the Sabbat Guide: Dark Thaumaturgy (Fires of the Inferno, Path of Phobos, Taking of the Spirit, and Dark Thaumaturgical Rituals), the Kiasyd Discipline Mytherceria, the Blood Brothers' Sanguinus, Superior Dementation, the Harbingers of Skulls' Mortuus Path and Necromantic Rituals, Superior Obtenebration, the Sabbat-specific Thaumaturgy paths of Mars and the Father's Vengeance (plus Sabbat Thaumaturgical Rituals), the Salubri antitribu's Valeren, and Superior Vicissitude. Also includes Camarilla Guide content: Visceratika (the Gargoyle bloodline's stone/earth Discipline — Skin of the Chameleon, Scry the Hearthstone, Bond with the Mountain, Armor of Terra, Flow Within the Mountain) and Gargoyle Flight (the Gargoyles' innate, non-Discipline flight ability, Basic/Intermediate/Advanced).
- `clans/` — Clan write-ups as Journal Entries. Includes `gargoyles.json`, covering the Tremere-bred Gargoyle servitor bloodline from the Camarilla Guide.
- `antitribu/` — Sabbat antitribu clans and unique Sabbat bloodlines (e.g. Assamite antitribu, Blood Brothers, Kiasyd, Panders, Salubri antitribu, Serpents of the Light) as Journal Entries, following the same schema as `clans/`
- `revenants/` — Sabbat revenant ghoul families as Journal Entries, same schema as `clans/`/`antitribu/`. Covers a general "what is a revenant" mechanics overview plus the four hereditary families still active in the Final Nights (Bratovitch, Grimaldi, Obertus, Zantosa), all Tzimisce-patronized, and a combined entry for szlachta and vohzd — the Tzimisce's individually-crafted (non-hereditary, NPC-only) specialized guardian and war ghouls, grouped here for thematic proximity even though they aren't a breeding lineage.
- `backgrounds/` — Background items (Allies, Contacts, Fame, Generation, Herd, Influence, Mentor, Resources, Retainers)
- `merits-flaws/` — Merit and Flaw items
- `rules-reference/` — Player-safe Journal entries for rules lookups: challenges (full trait-bidding mechanics), combat/weapons, archetypes, healing & damage, status, experience/advancement costs, the Sabbat ritae split across two files (`social-rituals.json` for Auctoritas Ritae — The Binding, Blood Bath, Blood Feast, Creation Rites, Festival of the Dead, Fire Dance, Games of Instinct, Grand Ball, Monomacy, Sermons of Caine, Vaulderie/Vinculum, War Party, Wild Hunt — and `ignoblis-ritae.json` for Low Rites — Acceptance, Allegiance, the Asp's Blessing, Contrition, Welcoming, Thanksgiving, Martial, Spilling of Blood, Stealth, Sun Dance, Tests of Pain, Truth Revealed), `code-of-milan.json` (the Sabbat's sect-wide oath of conduct, analogous to the Camarilla's Traditions), `traditions.json` (the Camarilla's own Six Traditions — the Masquerade, Domain, Progeny, Accounting, Hospitality, and Destruction — with each oath's text and the practical consequences of violating it), `justice-and-conclaves.json` (Camarilla justice mechanics beyond what's in `status.json`: princely trials and punishments by Tradition violated, the blood hunt/Lextalionis and its rules, archons operating as moles or showmen, the Red List and Anathema, alastors and the Trophy, and how grand/regional/judicial conclaves are convened and run), and `factions.json` (Sabbat political factions, treated by the book as being as important to a character's identity as her Nature — the Black Hand, the Sabbat Inquisition, the Loyalists, and the lesser factions: the Status Quo, the Moderates and their Pander Movement offshoot, the Ultra-Conservatives, the Old World Tzimisce and the associated Order of St. Blaise and Children of the Dracon, and the Infernalists. Each entry covers the faction's loyalty-vs-freedom political stance and what membership means socially for a character), and `master-influences.json` (Camarilla Guide elder-only Influence ratings 6-10 for Bureaucracy, Finance, High Society, Industry, Political, Transportation, University, and Underworld — summarizing the escalating city/regional/national-level power each tier grants, framed as requiring decades or centuries to attain), and `elders-of-the-camarilla.json` (Camarilla Guide "Elders of the Camarilla" chapter, extracted for mechanical/canon content only: the Morality Trait ceiling typical of true elders, confirmation that elder character creation uses the standard chargen rules with Generation as an ordinary Background Trait, guidance on elder-tier Discipline mastery and why elders reserve Master-level powers, elder Status norms, elder attitudes toward the Sabbat threat and toward neonates/ancillae, and clan-by-clan elder notes for Brujah, Malkavian, Nosferatu, Toreador, Tremere, and Ventrue — the only clans the source material covers for this chapter). Safe to expose to players.
- `rules-reference-st/` — Storyteller-only Journal entries (diablerie, derangements, frenzy/Rötschreck difficulty tables, feeding & hunger difficulty tables, `city-building.json`). Keep this compendium's visibility restricted to GMs — these cover ST-adjudicated material that players shouldn't be able to look up and game (e.g. exact frenzy/hunger difficulty numbers, derangement triggers). `city-building.json` (Camarilla Guide Chapter Five, "The City") extracts the chapter's two concrete numeric guidelines from an otherwise pure GM-worldbuilding chapter: the ~100,000-mortals-per-vampire feeding/overpopulation ratio, and the "Mapping the City" system for capping player Influence Trait levels and citywide Influence pools by category prevalence (poor/average/exceptional). The rest of the chapter (how to design a city's history, authorities, Elysium, hunting grounds, etc.) is Storyteller narrative-craft prose and was excluded per the project's established convention.
- `paths-of-enlightenment/` — Sabbat Paths of Enlightenment as Journal Entries (Caine, Cathari, Death and the Soul, Evil Revelations, the Feral Heart, Honorable Accord, Lilith, Power and the Inner Voice). Alternate Morality codes most Sabbat vampires follow instead of Humanity — no schema changes needed; a vampire following a Path just sets `morality.path` on their actor to the Path's name. Each entry documents the Path's two Virtues, its core philosophy, and a paraphrased hierarchy of sin ("Following the Path") describing the escalating acts that cost a Path Trait, mirroring how Humanity's hierarchy of sin works in the core book.
- `sabbat-npcs/` — the first Actor-type compendium in this system (all other packs above are Item or JournalEntry). Seven ready-to-play Sabbat NPC templates from the Sabbat Guide's closing chapter, as `type: "npc"` Actor documents: Archbishop, Bishop, Templar, Ductus, Priest, Grimaldi Fixer and Zantosa Socialite. Each includes Background/Roleplaying Hints prose plus a full Trait/Ability/Discipline/Background/Virtue/Path/Willpower/Status stat block, mapped onto the `VTMNpcData` schema (`scripts/documents/actor.mjs`): `system.concept/clan/bloodline/sect/generation/nature/demeanor/title`, `system.attributes.{physical,social,mental}.traits[]`, `system.negativeTraits[]`, `system.abilities.{talents,skills,knowledges}[]`, `system.willpower`, `system.morality` (Path of Enlightenment + rating), `system.virtues`, and `system.backgrounds[]`. Since the schema has no item-array population expected for this batch, full Discipline ratings and Status lists are kept as readable HTML in `system.biography` alongside the paraphrased Background/Roleplaying Hints text, rather than as Power items. The source text's OCR had some scrambling in these stat blocks (e.g. "Roht x2" read as "Robust x2," "Empathetic [N]" read as a Negative Trait per standard MET Trait lists) — cleaned up using judgment against standard Trait lists; no Traits were fabricated beyond what the source indicated. The two revenant ghoul templates (Grimaldi Fixer, Zantosa Socialite) use `npcType: "ghoul"` and have no listed Generation/Embrace year in the source.
- `camarilla-npcs/` — twelve ready-to-play NPC templates from the Camarilla Guide's closing "Portrait Gallery" chapter, as `type: "npc"` Actor documents following the same `VTMNpcData` mapping and biography-as-HTML approach as `sabbat-npcs/`: The Relentless Justicar (Malkavian), The Careful Archon (Toreador), The Weary Anarch (Brujah), The Committed Anarch (Brujah), The Bitter Ancilla (Tremere), The Frightened Elder (Ventrue) as `npcType: "vampire"`; The Adoring Ghoul, The Expendable Ghoul, The Realistic Ghoul as `npcType: "ghoul"`; and The Good Cop, The Cocky Thug, The Hapless Bystander as `npcType: "mortal"`. Unlike the Sabbat templates, the source text for this chapter has no separate "Background" prose section — only Roleplaying Hints plus the stat block — so `system.biography` contains just those two sections. The three mortal/ghoul templates in the source list no Generation, so `system.generation` (a required field with a minimum of 4) is left at the schema's default of 13 for them, and their Blood Pool is set to 0 for the mortals (Good Cop, Cocky Thug, Hapless Bystander have no Blood Pool listed in the source, being non-supernatural). The source book also has three further templates (The Innocent Neonate, The Nosy Reporter, The Frightened Child) not included in this batch. OCR in this chapter was largely legible; no Traits were fabricated, and none had to be omitted as illegible.

## Workflow

As each sourcebook is processed, the rules/content extracted from it is converted into JSON entries here, following the schema in `scripts/documents/item.mjs`. `potence-prowess.json` in `disciplines/` is a worked example of the expected shape for a `power` item.

Every document needs a unique 16-character alphanumeric `_id`. Generate one however is convenient (e.g. `foundry.utils.randomID()` in a Foundry console, or any 16-char random string) — it just needs to be stable and unique within the pack.
