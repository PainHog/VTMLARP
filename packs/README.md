# Compendium Source Data

Each subfolder here corresponds to a compendium pack declared in `system.json`. Foundry v12 reads these folders directly as "source" compendiums — one JSON file per document, no build/compile step required.

- `attributes/` — the 27 named Physical/Social/Mental Traits (e.g. Brawny, Charismatic, Clever) plus their Negative-Trait counterparts, as Attribute items (92 files: 20 positive + 10/11/10 negative per category)
- `abilities/` — the 30 core Abilities (Talents/Skills/Knowledges) as Ability items
- `disciplines/` — Discipline and Power items (drag onto a character's Powers tab). Includes Sabbat-sourced content from the Sabbat Guide: Dark Thaumaturgy (Fires of the Inferno, Path of Phobos, Taking of the Spirit, and Dark Thaumaturgical Rituals), the Kiasyd Discipline Mytherceria, the Blood Brothers' Sanguinus, Superior Dementation, the Harbingers of Skulls' Mortuus Path and Necromantic Rituals, Superior Obtenebration, the Sabbat-specific Thaumaturgy paths of Mars and the Father's Vengeance (plus Sabbat Thaumaturgical Rituals), the Salubri antitribu's Valeren, and Superior Vicissitude. Also includes Camarilla Guide content: Visceratika (the Gargoyle bloodline's stone/earth Discipline — Skin of the Chameleon, Scry the Hearthstone, Bond with the Mountain, Armor of Terra, Flow Within the Mountain) and Gargoyle Flight (the Gargoyles' innate, non-Discipline flight ability, Basic/Intermediate/Advanced). Also includes Dark Ages: Faith and Fire content (Chapter Four, Disciplines, second half — Dementation through Presence): for the seven Disciplines with modern-nights equivalents already in this pack (Dementation, Dominate, Fortitude, Obfuscate, Obtenebration, Potence, Presence), all files are prefixed `dark-ages-` and power names carry a "(Dark Ages)" suffix to keep them distinct from (and mechanically different than) their Revised-era counterparts (e.g. `dark-ages-dominate-vessel.json`, "Vessel (Dominate, Advanced, Dark Ages)"). Mortis (`mortis-*.json`) and Ogham (`ogham-*.json`) are unique to this circa-1230 sourcebook (Cappadocian/Lamia and Lhiannan Disciplines respectively) and have no modern-nights equivalent, so they use plain names. Mortis covers all three common paths a Cappadocian/Lamia practitioner may take as her primary path — Grave's Decay (Destroy the Husk, Rigor Mortis, Wither, Corrupt the Undead Flesh, Dissolve the Flesh), the Corpse in the Monster (Masque of Death, Cold of the Grave, Curse of Life, Gift of the Corpse, Gift of Life), and the Lamia-exclusive Path of the Four Humours (Whispers to the Soul, Kiss of the Dark Mother, Dark Humours, Clutching the Shroud, Black Breath) — plus four Basic/Advanced Mortis Rituals (Final Sight, Revivify the Cold Vitae, Eyes of the Grave, Walk Through the Shroud). No Combination Discipline content exists in this sourcebook's Chapter Four (the source text was searched specifically for "Combination Discipline" and returned no hits) — that system, and the existing `combination-discipline-*.json` files in this pack, come from the modern-nights Anarchs Guide instead. Also includes the first half of the same Faith and Fire Chapter Four (Abombwe through Daimoinon): Abombwe (`abombwe-*.json` — Predator's Communion, Caming the Beast, Whistling Up the Beast, Beast-Channel, Caring the Skin, Predator's Mastery), the secret Laibon Discipline, unique to this sourcebook with no modern-nights equivalent, uses plain names. Daimoinon (`daimoinon-*.json` — Sense the Sin, Fear of the Void, Essence of the Netherworld, Curse, Hell-Born Investiture), the Baali's infernal Discipline, is also unique and uses plain names; note two of its powers (Sense the Sin and Psychomachia) already existed from the Storytellers Guide, so this pass only added the four not already present. Animalism, Auspex, Celerity and Chimerstry all have modern-nights versions already in this pack, so their Dark Ages powers are prefixed `dark-ages-` and carry a "(Dark Ages)" name suffix (e.g. `dark-ages-auspex-soulsight.json`, "Soulsight (Auspex, Dark Ages, Basic)"): Animalism (Feral Speech, Noah's Call, Cowing the Beast, Ride the Wild Mind, Drawing Out the Beast, Quell the Herd), Auspex (Heightened Senses, Soulsight, The Spirit's Touch, Steal Secrets, Anima Walk, Farsight), Celerity (Alacrity, Swiftness, Rapidity, Legerity, Fleetness, Quickness), and Chimerstry (Ignis Fatuus, Ducomer, Apparition, Permanency, Horrid Reality, Mass Horror). Several of these Disciplines' highest tier is named "Elder" in the source text rather than "Advanced" (e.g. Predator's Mastery, Quell the Herd, Farsight, Mass Horror, Quickness, Hell-Born Investiture); since the `power` schema's `level` field only supports basic/intermediate/advanced, these are stored as `level: "advanced"` with the true tier noted in the item's display name (e.g. "Quickness (Celerity, Dark Ages, Elder)").
- `clans/` — Clan write-ups as Journal Entries. Includes `gargoyles.json`, covering the Tremere-bred Gargoyle servitor bloodline from the Camarilla Guide, and `dhampir.json`, covering the extremely rare half-mortal/half-vampire dhampirs from the Anarchs Guide (born of a fifteenth-generation vampire's mortal offspring) — their limited single-Basic-power-plus-Prowess Discipline access, Path of Humanity/mortal Virtues, Willpower and self-regenerating Blood Traits, and Freebie Trait allotment. Also includes four rare/independent bloodlines from the Storytellers Guide's "The Clans" chapter (its own opener describes three of these as bloodlines "the majority of Western Kindred know nothing about," best used as Storyteller characters): `baali.json` (Daimoinon/Obfuscate/Presence, the infernalist bloodline of demon-worshipping sages hunted by both sects), `nagaraja.json` (Auspex/Dominate/Necromancy, the flesh-eating necromancer "Flesh Eaters" reduced to a handful of survivors), `true-brujah.json` (Potence/Presence/Temporis, the "Elois" — cold, rationalist scholars who claim to be the true, unbroken bloodline of Carthage, distinct from the modern Brujah clan), and `old-clan-tzimisce.json` (Animalism/Auspex/Dominate, Tzimisce traditionalists who reject Vicissitude as a perversion and cling to feudal rule over isolated Eastern European estates). - `antitribu/` — Sabbat antitribu clans and unique Sabbat bloodlines (e.g. Assamite antitribu, Blood Brothers, Kiasyd, Panders, Salubri antitribu, Serpents of the Light) as Journal Entries, following the same schema as `clans/`

## Workflow

As each sourcebook is processed, the rules/content extracted from it is converted into JSON entries here, following the schema in `scripts/documents/item.mjs`. `potence-prowess.json` in `disciplines/` is a worked example of the expected shape for a `power` item.

Every document needs a unique 16-character alphanumeric `_id`. Generate one however is convenient (e.g. `foundry.utils.randomID()` in a Foundry console, or any 16-char random string) — it just needs to be stable and unique within the pack.
