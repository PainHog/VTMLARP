# Compendium Source Data

Each subfolder here corresponds to a compendium pack declared in `system.json`. Foundry v12 reads these folders directly as "source" compendiums — one JSON file per document, no build/compile step required.

- `attributes/` — the 27 named Physical/Social/Mental Traits (e.g. Brawny, Charismatic, Clever) plus their Negative-Trait counterparts, as Attribute items (92 files: 20 positive + 10/11/10 negative per category)
- `abilities/` — the 30 core Abilities (Talents/Skills/Knowledges) as Ability items
- `disciplines/` — Discipline and Power items (drag onto a character's Powers tab). Includes Sabbat-sourced content from the Sabbat Guide: Dark Thaumaturgy (Fires of the Inferno, Path of Phobos, Taking of the Spirit, and Dark Thaumaturgical Rituals), the Kiasyd Discipline Mytherceria, the Blood Brothers' Sanguinus, Superior Dementation, the Harbingers of Skulls' Mortuus Path and Necromantic Rituals, Superior Obtenebration, the Sabbat-specific Thaumaturgy paths of Mars and the Father's Vengeance (plus Sabbat Thaumaturgical Rituals), the Salubri antitribu's Valeren, and Superior Vicissitude. Also includes Camarilla Guide content: Visceratika (the Gargoyle bloodline's stone/earth Discipline — Skin of the Chameleon, Scry the Hearthstone, Bond with the Mountain, Armor of Terra, Flow Within the Mountain) and Gargoyle Flight (the Gargoyles' innate, non-Discipline flight ability, Basic/Intermediate/Advanced).
- `clans/` — Clan write-ups as Journal Entries. Includes `gargoyles.json`, covering the Tremere-bred Gargoyle servitor bloodline from the Camarilla Guide, and `dhampir.json`, covering the extremely rare half-mortal/half-vampire dhampirs from the Anarchs Guide (born of a fifteenth-generation vampire's mortal offspring) — their limited single-Basic-power-plus-Prowess Discipline access, Path of Humanity/mortal Virtues, Willpower and self-regenerating Blood Traits, and Freebie Trait allotment.
- `antitribu/` — Sabbat antitribu clans and unique Sabbat bloodlines (e.g. Assamite antitribu, Blood Brothers, Kiasyd, Panders, Salubri antitribu, Serpents of the Light) as Journal Entries, following the same schema as `clans/`
- `revenants/` — Sabbat revenant ghoul families as Journal Entries, same schema as `clans/`/`antitribu/`. Covers a general "what is a revenant" mechanics overview plus the four hereditary families still active in the Final Nights (Bratovitch, Grimaldi, Obertus, Zantosa), all Tzimisce-patronized, and a combined entry for szlachta and vohzd — the Tzimisce's individually-crafted (non-hereditary, NPC-only) specialized guardian and war ghouls, grouped here for thematic proximity even though they aren't a breeding lineage.
- `backgrounds/` — Background items (Allies, Contacts, Fame, Generation, Herd, Influence, Mentor, Resources, Retainers). Also includes `insight.json` from the Anarchs Guide, an exclusive Background only the thin-blooded Last Generations may take, covering the involuntary/voluntary/group Insight rules and its interactions with torpor, Auspex and Dementation.
- `merits-flaws/` — Merit and Flaw items. Includes several new-Merits-and-Flaws entries from the Anarchs Guide "The Last Generations" and "Dhampirs" sections: `flaw-fifteenth-generation.json` (companion to the pre-existing `flaw-fourteenth-generation.json` and `flaw-thin-blood.json`), `merit-personal-masquerade.json`, `flaw-ravaging-years.json`, `flaw-hemetic.json`, `flaw-clan-weakness.json`, `flaw-decrepitude.json`, `flaw-fangless.json`, and `flaw-superstition.json` (thin-blood/Last-Generations Merits and Flaws), plus `merit-perceive-vampires.json` and `flaw-fragile-bones.json`, two Merits/Flaws exclusive to dhampir characters (see `clans/dhampir.json`).
- `rules-reference/` — Player-safe Journal entries for rules lookups: challenges (full trait-bidding mechanics), combat/weapons, archetypes, healing & damage, status, experience/advancement costs, the Sabbat ritae split across two files (`social-rituals.json` for Auctoritas Ritae — The Binding, Blood Bath, Blood Feast, Creation Rites, Festival of the Dead, Fire Dance, Games of Instinct, Grand Ball, Monomacy, Sermons of Caine, Vaulderie/Vinculum, War Party, Wild Hunt — and `ignoblis-ritae.json` for Low Rites — Acceptance, Allegiance, the Asp's Blessing, Contrition, Welcoming, Thanksgiving, Martial, Spilling of Blood, Stealth, Sun Dance, Tests of Pain, Truth Revealed), `code-of-milan.json` (the Sabbat's sect-wide oath of conduct, analogous to the Camarilla's Traditions), `traditions.json` (the Camarilla's own Six Traditions — the Masquerade, Domain, Progeny, Accounting, Hospitality, and Destruction — with each oath's text and the practical consequences of violating it), `justice-and-conclaves.json` (Camarilla justice mechanics beyond what's in `status.json`: princely trials and punishments by Tradition violated, the blood hunt/Lextalionis and its rules, archons operating as moles or showmen, the Red List and Anathema, alastors and the Trophy, and how grand/regional/judicial conclaves are convened and run), and `factions.json` (Sabbat political factions, treated by the book as being as important to a character's identity as her Nature — the Black Hand, the Sabbat Inquisition, the Loyalists, and the lesser factions: the Status Quo, the Moderates and their Pander Movement offshoot, the Ultra-Conservatives, the Old World Tzimisce and the associated Order of St. Blaise and Children of the Dracon, and the Infernalists. Each entry covers the faction's loyalty-vs-freedom political stance and what membership means socially for a character), and `master-influences.json` (Camarilla Guide elder-only Influence ratings 6-10 for Bureaucracy, Finance, High Society, Industry, Political, Transportation, University, and Underworld — summarizing the escalating city/regional/national-level power each tier grants, framed as requiring decades or centuries to attain), `elders-of-the-camarilla.json` (Camarilla Guide "Elders of the Camarilla" chapter, extracted for mechanical/canon content only: the Morality Trait ceiling typical of true elders, confirmation that elder character creation uses the standard chargen rules with Generation as an ordinary Background Trait, guidance on elder-tier Discipline mastery and why elders reserve Master-level powers, elder Status norms, elder attitudes toward the Sabbat threat and toward neonates/ancillae, and clan-by-clan elder notes for Brujah, Malkavian, Nosferatu, Toreador, Tremere, and Ventrue — the only clans the source material covers for this chapter), and `anarch-character-creation.json` (Anarchs Guide Chapter Two, "Taking Up the Fight" — the anarch-specific spin on the standard five-step character creation process: concept/personality/clan-distribution guidance, Attribute and Ability purchase caveats, leniency guidance for out-of-clan Disciplines, the split between easy "Common Ties" Backgrounds like Allies/Contacts/Retainers versus contested "Uncommon Ties" like Herd/Generation/Influence/Resources, Blood/Willpower/Morality/Virtue assignment guidance, Negative Trait/Flaw/Derangement guidance flagging which Flaws are common or discouraged for anarchs, Merit guidance, and Free Trait mechanics. This chapter's chargen text pointed back to the core rules for all actual Trait costs and mechanics and introduced no new Merits, Flaws or Backgrounds as distinct items — only GM guidance on applying existing ones to anarch characters — so no new merit/flaw/background files were needed from it. The chapter's latter half (anarch Discipline-usage color commentary, new combination Disciplines, and Anarch Thaumaturgy rituals) is Discipline/Power content out of scope for this file and reserved for the `disciplines/` pack), and `status-perfectus.json` (Anarchs Guide Chapter One, "History in Ashes" — this chapter is otherwise pure historical narrative recounting the 14th-century Anarch Revolt, the Convention of Thorns, and the rise and fall of the 1944-2000 Los Angeles Anarch Free State, and was excluded per the project's established convention on narrative-craft/lore chapters, but the chapter defines one genuinely reusable piece of in-universe canon: the Status Perfectus, the Anarch Movement's closest equivalent to a founding charter, analogous to the Camarilla's Traditions or the Sabbat's Code of Milan. This file documents its origin (drafted by the Revolutionary Council after the Second Anarch Revolt of 1944), its four core tenets, and its non-binding, aspirational status at the table. Chapters Four ("Radical Thinking") and Five ("So You Want a Revolution") were also scanned for hidden mechanics and found to contain none extractable — both are pure player psychology/roleplaying guidance and Storyteller chronicle-design advice respectively, with no new numeric systems, difficulties, or named mechanics distinct from what's already in the core rules, so no files were built from them), and `thin-blooded.json` (Anarchs Guide Chapter Three, "The Rebel Unlife" — "The Last Generations": what defines fourteenth/fifteenth-generation thin-blooded vampires, the Book of Nod's Gehenna-harbinger prophecy about them, and how the sects versus the Anarch Movement treat them; the thin-blood-specific character-creation deltas from standard vampire chargen — six-Trait primary Attribute category, mandatory Generation Flaw instead of the Generation Background, restricted Basic/Intermediate-only Discipline access gated by clan for fourteenth-generation characters, Background guidance including the new Insight Background, and Merit/Flaw fit guidance — plus the "Anomalous Biological Activity and Aging" and "Waking Up" rules and roleplaying-a-thin-blood guidance), `anarch-factions.json` (Anarchs Guide Chapter Three, "The Rebel Unlife" — the Movement's ideological spectrum of political impulses, from the Loyal Opposition still working inside the Camarilla (Sympathizers, Reformers, Constructionists) through the true Disloyal Opposition (Radicals, Militants, Hardliners) to the unaligned Anarch Fringe often mistaken for anarchs proper (Autarchs/Autarkis, Mercenaries, Nihilists), plus an overview of how anarchs by choice differ from "accidental" anarchs, including the Sabbat-refugee and enlightened-elder special cases. Each entry covers the group's core philosophy and what it means socially/mechanically for a character), and `anarch-territory-and-structure.json` (the same chapter's territory, feeding and gang-organization material — anarch-specific feeding/poaching consequences, the anarch vocabulary for the Blood Bond as "Blood Slavery" and anarch norms around ghoul-keeping, how Anarch Free Territories organize differently depending on whether they're Enemy, Disputed, or fully anarch-controlled Territories, the consent-based Baron and Councilor leadership roles plus the informally-titled Other Officers (ambassadors, counters, deputies, moles), and Anarch Gang structure by size (urban/small, larger multi-set, largest/general-and-captain) and type (settled, nomadic, guerrilla/terrorist cell), plus the Prospect/Cousin/Initiate membership pipeline), and `anarch-reputation.json` (Anarchs Guide Chapter Six, "Faces in the Crowd" — the Anarch Reputation system, the Movement's informal, deed-based parallel to Camarilla and Sabbat Status: how Reputation differs mechanically from sect Status (no penalty for anarchs ignoring it, never applies against sect vampires, doesn't decide disciplinary/criminal proceedings), the temporary-Reputation informal-vote mechanic and the permanent-Reputation full-gathering vote mechanic (thumbs up/down/fist, 2/3 majority to award vs. simple majority to remove/assign negative, Subterfuge-concealed voting), anarch-style boons and prestation, the full list of named positive Reputation Traits (Advocate/Believer/Champion/Preacher, Bond/Bond Girl, Committed, Hardcore, Loaded/Hook-Up/Connected, Marked, Soldier/Warrior/Veteran/Lifer, Tested, True Believer, Visionary) and negative Reputation Traits (Burnt Out, Casualty, Cherry, Defeated, Overzealous, Thug, Unenlightened, Unreliable, Vain), and closing GM guidance on keeping Reputation's inherent subjectivity from curdling into real-table hard feelings). Safe to expose to players.
- `rules-reference-st/` — Storyteller-only Journal entries (diablerie, derangements, frenzy/Rötschreck difficulty tables, feeding & hunger difficulty tables, `city-building.json`). Keep this compendium's visibility restricted to GMs — these cover ST-adjudicated material that players shouldn't be able to look up and game (e.g. exact frenzy/hunger difficulty numbers, derangement triggers). `city-building.json` (Camarilla Guide Chapter Five, "The City") extracts the chapter's two concrete numeric guidelines from an otherwise pure GM-worldbuilding chapter: the ~100,000-mortals-per-vampire feeding/overpopulation ratio, and the "Mapping the City" system for capping player Influence Trait levels and citywide Influence pools by category prevalence (poor/average/exceptional). The rest of the chapter (how to design a city's history, authorities, Elysium, hunting grounds, etc.) is Storyteller narrative-craft prose and was excluded per the project's established convention.
- `paths-of-enlightenment/` — Sabbat Paths of Enlightenment as Journal Entries (Caine, Cathari, Death and the Soul, Evil Revelations, the Feral Heart, Honorable Accord, Lilith, Power and the Inner Voice). Alternate Morality codes most Sabbat vampires follow instead of Humanity — no schema changes needed; a vampire following a Path just sets `morality.path` on their actor to the Path's name. Each entry documents the Path's two Virtues, its core philosophy, and a paraphrased hierarchy of sin ("Following the Path") describing the escalating acts that cost a Path Trait, mirroring how Humanity's hierarchy of sin works in the core book.
- `sabbat-npcs/` — the first Actor-type compendium in this system (all other packs above are Item or JournalEntry). Seven ready-to-play Sabbat NPC templates from the Sabbat Guide's closing chapter, as `type: "npc"` Actor documents: Archbishop, Bishop, Templar, Ductus, Priest, Grimaldi Fixer and Zantosa Socialite. Each includes Background/Roleplaying Hints prose plus a full Trait/Ability/Discipline/Background/Virtue/Path/Willpower/Status stat block, mapped onto the `VTMNpcData` schema (`scripts/documents/actor.mjs`): `system.concept/clan/bloodline/sect/generation/nature/demeanor/title`, `system.attributes.{physical,social,mental}.traits[]`, `system.negativeTraits[]`, `system.abilities.{talents,skills,knowledges}[]`, `system.willpower`, `system.morality` (Path of Enlightenment + rating), `system.virtues`, and `system.backgrounds[]`. Since the schema has no item-array population expected for this batch, full Discipline ratings and Status lists are kept as readable HTML in `system.biography` alongside the paraphrased Background/Roleplaying Hints text, rather than as Power items. The source text's OCR had some scrambling in these stat blocks (e.g. "Roht x2" read as "Robust x2," "Empathetic [N]" read as a Negative Trait per standard MET Trait lists) — cleaned up using judgment against standard Trait lists; no Traits were fabricated beyond what the source indicated. The two revenant ghoul templates (Grimaldi Fixer, Zantosa Socialite) use `npcType: "ghoul"` and have no listed Generation/Embrace year in the source.
- `camarilla-npcs/` — fifteen ready-to-play NPC templates from the Camarilla Guide's closing "Portrait Gallery" chapter, as `type: "npc"` Actor documents following the same `VTMNpcData` mapping and biography-as-HTML approach as `sabbat-npcs/`: The Relentless Justicar (Malkavian), The Careful Archon (Toreador), The Weary Anarch (Brujah), The Committed Anarch (Brujah), The Bitter Ancilla (Tremere), The Frightened Elder (Ventrue), The Innocent Neonate (Toreador) as `npcType: "vampire"`; The Adoring Ghoul, The Expendable Ghoul, The Realistic Ghoul as `npcType: "ghoul"`; and The Good Cop, The Cocky Thug, The Hapless Bystander, The Nosy Reporter, The Frightened Child as `npcType: "mortal"`. Unlike the Sabbat templates, the source text for this chapter has no separate "Background" prose section — only Roleplaying Hints plus the stat block — so `system.biography` contains just those two sections. Templates with no listed Generation in the source leave `system.generation` (a required field with a minimum of 4) at the schema's default of 13, and mortals have a Blood Pool of 0. OCR in this chapter was largely legible; no Traits were fabricated, and none had to be omitted as illegible.
- `anarch-npcs/` — eleven ready-to-play NPC templates from the Anarchs Guide's own "Faces in the Crowd" Portrait Gallery (Chapter Six), as `type: "npc"` Actor documents following the same `VTMNpcData`/biography-as-HTML approach as `sabbat-npcs/` and `camarilla-npcs/`: The Baron (Toreador), The Emissary (Brujah), Chameleon (Caitiff posing as Malkavian), Smuggler (Gangrel), The Networker (Malkavian), Nomadic Pack Leader (Caitiff), Ex-Sabbat Recruit (Lasombra), Rogue Warlock (Tremere), The Mistake (Caitiff, thin-blooded — OCR-garbled as "THE MITAKE" in the source scan), and The Unruly (Nosferatu), all `npcType: "vampire"`, plus The Obsessed Ghoul-Friend as `npcType: "ghoul"` (no Clan/Generation/Disciplines listed in the source beyond a single Potence dot gained via her domitor's vitae). Two further headers in this chapter's page range, Baron and Councilor, are leadership-role descriptions (not character stat blocks) and were intentionally not built as NPCs — see `rules-reference/anarch-territory-and-structure.json` for that material instead. OCR in this chapter was heavily scrambled in places (garbled Trait/Ability names, stray "(N)" negative-Trait markers, page-header noise reading "FAC'ACES IN THE CROWD"); Traits and Abilities were reconstructed against standard MET Trait/Ability lists using judgment, with no Trait fabricated beyond what the source clearly intended.

## Workflow

As each sourcebook is processed, the rules/content extracted from it is converted into JSON entries here, following the schema in `scripts/documents/item.mjs`. `potence-prowess.json` in `disciplines/` is a worked example of the expected shape for a `power` item.

Every document needs a unique 16-character alphanumeric `_id`. Generate one however is convenient (e.g. `foundry.utils.randomID()` in a Foundry console, or any 16-char random string) — it just needs to be stable and unique within the pack.
