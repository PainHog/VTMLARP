# Compendium Source Data

Each subfolder here corresponds to a compendium pack declared in `system.json`. Foundry v12 reads these folders directly as "source" compendiums — one JSON file per document, no build/compile step required.

- `attributes/` — the 27 named Physical/Social/Mental Traits (e.g. Brawny, Charismatic, Clever) plus their Negative-Trait counterparts, as Attribute items (92 files: 20 positive + 10/11/10 negative per category)
- `abilities/` — the 30 core Abilities (Talents/Skills/Knowledges) as Ability items
- `disciplines/` — Discipline and Power items (drag onto a character's Powers tab). Also includes Camarilla Guide content: Visceratika (the Gargoyle bloodline's stone/earth Discipline — Skin of the Chameleon, Scry the Hearthstone, Bond with the Mountain, Armor of Terra, Flow Within the Mountain) and Gargoyle Flight (the Gargoyles' innate, non-Discipline flight ability, Basic/Intermediate/Advanced). Also includes Dark Ages: Faith and Fire content (Chapter Four, Disciplines, second half — Dementation through Presence): for the seven Disciplines with modern-nights equivalents already in this pack (Dementation, Dominate, Fortitude, Obfuscate, Obtenebration, Potence, Presence), all files are prefixed `dark-ages-` and power names carry a "(Dark Ages)" suffix to keep them distinct from (and mechanically different than) their Revised-era counterparts (e.g. `dark-ages-dominate-vessel.json`, "Vessel (Dominate, Advanced, Dark Ages)"). Mortis (`mortis-*.json`) and Ogham (`ogham-*.json`) are unique to this circa-1230 sourcebook (Cappadocian/Lamia and Lhiannan Disciplines respectively) and have no modern-nights equivalent, so they use plain names. Mortis covers all three common paths a Cappadocian/Lamia practitioner may take as her primary path — Grave's Decay (Destroy the Husk, Rigor Mortis, Wither, Corrupt the Undead Flesh, Dissolve the Flesh), the Corpse in the Monster (Masque of Death, Cold of the Grave, Curse of Life, Gift of the Corpse, Gift of Life), and the Lamia-exclusive Path of the Four Humours (Whispers to the Soul, Kiss of the Dark Mother, Dark Humours, Clutching the Shroud, Black Breath) — plus four Basic/Advanced Mortis Rituals (Final Sight, Revivify the Cold Vitae, Eyes of the Grave, Walk Through the Shroud). ## Workflow

As each sourcebook is processed, the rules/content extracted from it is converted into JSON entries here, following the schema in `scripts/documents/item.mjs`. `potence-prowess.json` in `disciplines/` is a worked example of the expected shape for a `power` item.

Every document needs a unique 16-character alphanumeric `_id`. Generate one however is convenient (e.g. `foundry.utils.randomID()` in a Foundry console, or any 16-char random string) — it just needs to be stable and unique within the pack.

## Dark Ages: Vampire ("Faith and Fire") content

`Faith and Fire` is a Dark Ages Vampire sourcebook (a revised edition of "The Long Night") set circa 1230 AD, using different terminology than the modern-nights books above: High Clans/Low Clans instead of the 13-clan structure, Roads instead of Paths of Enlightenment, and medieval princely Courts instead of Camarilla/Sabbat/Anarch.
