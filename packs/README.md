# Compendium Source Data

Each subfolder here corresponds to a compendium pack declared in `system.json`. Foundry v12 reads these folders directly as "source" compendiums — one JSON file per document, no build/compile step required.

- `attributes/` — the 27 named Physical/Social/Mental Traits (e.g. Brawny, Charismatic, Clever) plus their Negative-Trait counterparts, as Attribute items (92 files: 20 positive + 10/11/10 negative per category)
- `abilities/` — the 30 core Abilities (Talents/Skills/Knowledges) as Ability items
- `disciplines/` — Discipline and Power items (drag onto a character's Powers tab). Includes Sabbat-sourced content from the Sabbat Guide: Dark Thaumaturgy (Fires of the Inferno, Path of Phobos, Taking of the Spirit, and Dark Thaumaturgical Rituals), the Kiasyd Discipline Mytherceria, the Blood Brothers' Sanguinus, Superior Dementation, the Harbingers of Skulls' Mortuus Path and Necromantic Rituals, Superior Obtenebration, the Sabbat-specific Thaumaturgy paths of Mars and the Father's Vengeance (plus Sabbat Thaumaturgical Rituals), the Salubri antitribu's Valeren, and Superior Vicissitude. Also includes Camarilla Guide content: Visceratika (the Gargoyle bloodline's stone/earth Discipline — Skin of the Chameleon, Scry the Hearthstone, Bond with the Mountain, Armor of Terra, Flow Within the Mountain) and Gargoyle Flight (the Gargoyles' innate, non-Discipline flight ability, Basic/Intermediate/Advanced). Also includes Dark Ages: Faith and Fire content (Chapter Four, Disciplines, second half — Dementation through Presence): for the seven Disciplines with modern-nights equivalents already in this pack (Dementation, Dominate, Fortitude, Obfuscate, Obtenebration, Potence, Presence), all files are prefixed `dark-ages-` and power names carry a "(Dark Ages)" suffix to keep them distinct from (and mechanically different than) their Revised-era counterparts (e.g. `dark-ages-dominate-vessel.json`, "Vessel (Dominate, Advanced, Dark Ages)"). Mortis (`mortis-*.json`) and Ogham (`ogham-*.json`) are unique to this circa-1230 sourcebook (Cappadocian/Lamia and Lhiannan Disciplines respectively) and have no modern-nights equivalent, so they use plain names. Mortis covers all three common paths a Cappadocian/Lamia practitioner may take as her primary path — Grave's Decay (Destroy the Husk, Rigor Mortis, Wither, Corrupt the Undead Flesh, Dissolve the Flesh), the Corpse in the Monster (Masque of Death, Cold of the Grave, Curse of Life, Gift of the Corpse, Gift of Life), and the Lamia-exclusive Path of the Four Humours (Whispers to the Soul, Kiss of the Dark Mother, Dark Humours, Clutching the Shroud, Black Breath) — plus four Basic/Advanced Mortis Rituals (Final Sight, Revivify the Cold Vitae, Eyes of the Grave, Walk Through the Shroud). Daimoinon (`daimoinon-*.json` — Sense the Sin, Fear of the Void, Essence of the Netherworld, Curse, Hell-Born Investiture), the Baali's infernal Discipline, is also unique and uses plain names; note two of its powers (Sense the Sin and Psychomachia) already existed from the Storytellers Guide, so this pass only added the four not already present. Animalism, Auspex, Celerity and Chimerstry all have modern-nights versions already in this pack, so their Dark Ages powers are prefixed `dark-ages-` and carry a "(Dark Ages)" name suffix (e.g. `dark-ages-auspex-soulsight.json`, "Soulsight (Auspex, Dark Ages, Basic)"): Animalism (Feral Speech, Noah's Call, Cowing the Beast, Ride the Wild Mind, Drawing Out the Beast, Quell the Herd), Auspex (Heightened Senses, Soulsight, The Spirit's Touch, Steal Secrets, Anima Walk, Farsight), Celerity (Alacrity, Swiftness, Rapidity, Legerity, Fleetness, Quickness), and Chimerstry (Ignis Fatuus, Ducomer, Apparition, Permanency, Horrid Reality, Mass Horror). ## Workflow

As each sourcebook is processed, the rules/content extracted from it is converted into JSON entries here, following the schema in `scripts/documents/item.mjs`. `potence-prowess.json` in `disciplines/` is a worked example of the expected shape for a `power` item.

Every document needs a unique 16-character alphanumeric `_id`. Generate one however is convenient (e.g. `foundry.utils.randomID()` in a Foundry console, or any 16-char random string) — it just needs to be stable and unique within the pack.

## Dark Ages: Vampire ("Faith and Fire") content

`Faith and Fire` is a Dark Ages Vampire sourcebook (a revised edition of "The Long Night") set circa 1230 AD, using different terminology than the modern-nights books above: High Clans/Low Clans instead of the 13-clan structure, Roads instead of Paths of Enlightenment, and medieval princely Courts instead of Camarilla/Sabbat/Anarch. All entries from this book are tagged "(Dark Ages)" in their display names to distinguish them from modern-nights content sharing the same folders.

- `rules-reference/dark-ages-traditions.json` — "The Traditions of Caine (Dark Ages)": the six Traditions (Covenant, Domain, Progeny, Accounting, Destruction, Silence of the Blood) with each oath's text and practical meaning in the Dark Medieval feudal setting.
- `rules-reference/dark-ages-crime-and-punishment.json` — "Crime and Punishment: Lextalionis (Dark Ages)": the Lextalionis justice system, the blood hunt procedure, and lesser punishments (branding, exile/Autarkis status, and the four common ordeal types).
- `rules-reference/dark-ages-courts.json` — "The Courts of Princes (Dark Ages)": princely rights/responsibilities, the six court officer posts (sheriff, warmaster, seneschal, keeper, chamberlain, scourge), the Feudal Lextalionis rank structure (monarch/lord/prince/baron/knight) and fealty mechanics, plus a short High Clans/Low Clans structural summary.
- `rules-reference-st/dark-ages-sects-and-war-of-princes.json` — Storyteller-only: the secretive sects (Inconnu, Order of Bitter Ashes, Furores, Prometheans, Cainite Heresy) and the War of Princes' origins and active fronts (the Crusades, the Eastern Marches/Omen War, the Inquisition, the Shadow Reconquista, the rising Low Clans), kept ST-only since it's GM-facing chronicle/antagonist-framing material rather than player-facing rules.

Excluded as pure narrative/worldbuilding/flavor text (per established convention): the "Stories Around the Fire" MET framing essay, "What is Mind's Eye Theatre," the Storyteller/character-creation pep-talk sections, "The Rules Thou Shalt Never Break" (real-world safety guidance, not game mechanics), the "World Lit Only by Fire" historical-timeline prose, the Caine/Embrace/Beast/Disciplines/Generations lore essays (color/setting text with no distinct new mechanics beyond what's already covered by existing clan/Discipline/Generation content), the Roads intro paragraph (the five Roads themselves are reserved for a future dedicated Roads-of-Dark-Ages file when their full hierarchies-of-sin are reached later in the book), the Lexicon (a glossary, not a mechanic), and the closing in-fiction vignette.

From Chapter Three ("Character Creation"), which covers the full Dark Ages chargen process (Attributes, Abilities, Disciplines, Backgrounds, Blood, Willpower, Virtues, Roads, Merits & Flaws, Experience). Faith and Fire's Attribute, Ability and Background lists are almost entirely identical to the modern-nights lists already in `attributes/`, `abilities/` and `backgrounds/`, so only genuinely new/period-specific content was added, all tagged "(Dark Ages)":

- `abilities/ability-archery-dark-ages.json`, `ability-hearth-wisdom-dark-ages.json`, `ability-ride-dark-ages.json`, `ability-seneschal-dark-ages.json`, `ability-theology-dark-ages.json` — five Dark Ages Abilities with no modern-nights equivalent already in `abilities/` (the modern list instead has Firearms/Drive/Computer/Security/Science/Finance/Streetwise/Awareness, which don't fit 1230 AD). All other Faith and Fire Abilities (Academics, Alertness, Animal Ken, Athletics, Brawl, Commerce/Finance, Crafts, Dodge, Empathy, Etiquette, Expression, Hobby/Professional Expert, Intimidation, Investigation, Law, Leadership, Legerdemain, Linguistics, Medicine, Melee, Occult, Performance, Politics, Repair, Scrounge, Stealth, Subterfuge, Survival) matched existing entries and were skipped as duplicates.
- No new Backgrounds were needed — Faith and Fire's Background list (Allies, Contacts, Domain, Generation, Herd, Influence, Mentor, Resources, Retainers) is identical to what's already in `backgrounds/`.
- `merits-flaws/merit-celestial-attunement-dark-ages.json`, `merit-berserker-dark-ages.json`, `merit-crusader-dark-ages.json`, `merit-pitiable-dark-ages.json`, `merit-danger-sense-dark-ages.json`, `merit-faerie-affinity-dark-ages.json` — six new period-specific Merits. Several Faith and Fire Merits (Common Sense, Eat Food/"Cat Food", Iron Will, Concentration, Lucky, Nine Lives, Magic Resistance, Medium, True Love, Debt of Gratitude, Natural Leader, Prestigious Sire, Ambidextrous, Acute Senses, Catlike Balance, Blush of Health, Daredevil, Huge Size, Efficient Digestion, Calm Heart, Code of Honor, Eidetic Memory, Light Sleeper) exactly duplicate Merits already in `merits-flaws/` and were skipped.
- `merits-flaws/flaw-ragged-bite-dark-ages.json`, `flaw-leper-dark-ages.json`, `flaw-religious-prohibition-dark-ages.json`, `flaw-flesh-eater-dark-ages.json`, `flaw-infamous-sire-dark-ages.json`, `flaw-cannot-embrace-dark-ages.json`, `flaw-thirteenth-generation-dark-ages.json`, `flaw-cursed-dark-ages.json` — eight new period-specific Flaws. Many other Faith and Fire Flaws (Hard of Hearing, Short, Bad Sight, Disfigured, One Eye, Leper's contagious variant aside, Monstrous, Permanent Wound, Slow Healing, Child, Deaf, Flesh of the Corpse, Mute, Blind, Deep Sleeper, Nightmares, Prey Exclusion, Soft-Hearted, Amnesia, Phobia, Territorial, Short-Fuse, Dark Secret, Mistaken Identity, Shy, Sire's Resentment, Enemy, Hunted, Addiction, Smell of the Grave, Diseased/Disease Carrier, Hatred, Vengeful) match Flaws already present under the same or near-identical names and were skipped as duplicates.
- `paths-of-enlightenment/road-of-beast.json`, `road-of-heaven.json`, `road-of-humanity.json`, `road-of-kings.json`, `road-of-sin.json` — the five core Roads (Via Bestiae, Via Caeli, Via Humanitatis, Via Regalis, Via Peccati), Dark Ages' equivalent of Paths of Enlightenment, built as JournalEntry documents in the same folder (a vampire follows a Road by setting `morality.path` on their actor, same as a Sabbat Path — no schema change needed). Each entry documents its governing Virtues, Aura of Conviction, core Ethics, and paraphrased Hierarchy of Sins. Clan-specific minor roads (Via Sanguinis, Via Ossium, Via Mutationis, Via Noctis, Via Paradoxa, Via Serpentis) are mentioned by the source only in passing, with no mechanical detail given beyond a name and associated clan, and were not built as separate entries.
- `rules-reference/dark-ages-character-creation.json` — "Dark Ages Character Creation (Faith and Fire)": the purely mechanical chargen deltas from modern-nights Laws of the Night — the seven/five/three Attribute-priority allocation, the Virtue/Road-Trait math (Road rating = average of Conscience/Conviction and Self-Control/Instinct, rounded up) and the character-creation-only option to trade one Road Trait for two Free Traits, the full Free Trait cost table, Merit/Flaw/Negative-Trait/derangement caps, the Twelfth-Generation starting-Generation norm, and Dark-Ages-specific Experience notes (Road Trait advancement cost, and buying a Merit specifically to narratively overcome an existing Flaw at double cost). Narrative hand-holding ("what is a concept," the worked Frederic von Aarlsburgh sample character, Archetype descriptions already covered by `rules-reference/archetypes.json`) was excluded as out of scope.

From Faith and Fire Chapters Five through Seven (Rules/Systems/Drama; Storytelling; Allies and Antagonists). The core Trait-bidding challenge system, combat sequence, health/wound tracks, frenzy/Rötschreck, diablerie and Status/Prestation mechanics described in Chapter Five are mechanically **identical** to the modern-nights rules already documented in `rules-reference/challenges.json`, `combat-weapons.json`, `healing-damage.json`, `rules-reference-st/frenzy-rotschreck.json`, `rules-reference-st/diablerie.json`, and `rules-reference/status.json` — so this pass did not re-document them. Instead it extracted the genuine Dark Ages-specific deltas and additions, prefixed `dark-ages-` and tagged "(Dark Ages)" in each entry's display name:

- `rules-reference/dark-ages-weapons-and-armor.json` — the circa-1230 weapon/armor/shield card tables (melee, thrown, ranged), the two-weapon-fighting rules (distinct from the modern Ambidextrous/Florentine rules already on file), and full mounted-combat rules (steed control, charging, lances, horse archery, trampling) — none of which exist in the modern `combat-weapons.json`.
- `rules-reference/dark-ages-health-and-disease.json` — mortal wound recovery-time tables (weeks/months rather than modern medicine's days), the period-specific disease list (dysentery, ergotism, idropesie, leprosy, plague, syphilis, tuberculosis, Saint Vitus's Dance) with their Cainite-vector and vitae-purging mechanics, and Dark Medieval drugs/poisons and their effects on a feeding vampire.
- `rules-reference/dark-ages-true-faith.json` — True Faith's full Dark Ages toolkit: repelling vampires with a holy symbol, blessing items and holy water, relics, the Gifts of Faith difficulty table (banishing ghosts/demons, warding Dominate/Presence, severing blood oaths), and holy ground effects — expanding well beyond the existing `merit-true-faith.json` merit stub.
- `rules-reference/dark-ages-blood-oath-and-golconda.json` — the three-drink blood oath progression (First/Second/Third Drink mechanical effects) and how the bond is resisted or broken, plus the Golconda/Bitter Journey framework and its five potential rewards (Acceptance, Calm, Hunger, Strength, Mortality).
- `rules-reference/dark-ages-ghouls-and-revenants.json` — Dark Ages ghoul-creation and animal-ghoul rules, plus the six Tzimisce revenant families (Bratovitch, Grimaldi, Krevcheski, Obertus, Santovich, Vlasy) with their inherited Disciplines and weaknesses — a companion to (not a replacement for) the existing `revenants/` pack, which covers the Sabbat-era Final Nights revenant families instead.
- `rules-reference-st/dark-ages-antagonist-types.json` — Chapter Seven's antagonist **templates** (not named NPCs): demons/spirits (Willpower/Rage/Gnosis/Essence Traits and Charms), Lupines (hybrid battle-form, Rage, Gnosis), wizards/sorcerers (the three practitioner types), ghosts (anchors, detection, powers), and the Dark Ages fae (Seelie/Unseelie, Glamour, cold iron), plus Soulsight detection notes and the cross-type rules for drinking other supernaturals' blood or attempting to Embrace them. Chapter Seven contains no named sample characters, so no new Actor pack was created for it — `packs/dark-ages-npcs/` does not exist and `system.json` was not modified.
- `rules-reference-st/dark-ages-torpor-and-deterioration.json` — Storyteller-only torpor duration by Road rating, the staking/unstaking mechanics, and blood-deterioration rules for starved or imprisoned vampires.
- `rules-reference-st/dark-ages-church-and-inquisition.json` — canon institution notes on Mother Church, the Templars, the nascent 1230-era Inquisition, and other faiths (Judaism, paganism, Islam, divergent Christian sects) as antagonist/plot-hook material for Storytellers.

Chapter Six ("Storytelling") was scanned in full and found to be pure Storyteller-craft prose (theme/mood/pacing/scale/scope guidance, running sessions, troubleshooting problem players, story seeds) with no extractable dice mechanics, tables, or reusable named systems distinct from ordinary GM advice, and was excluded per the project's established convention on narrative-craft chapters.

- `rules-reference/elder-character-creation.json` — "Elder Character Creation (Elder)", from Laws of Elysium's Introduction and Character Creation chapters (a modern-nights sourcebook on playing 250+-year-old elder vampires, not to be confused with the Dark Ages material elsewhere in this pack). Documents only the mechanical deltas from standard chargen: the 10/8/6 (rather than standard) Attribute priority split, 8 starting Abilities, 6 starting Disciplines, 6 starting Influences, the rule that Master-level Discipline powers are purchasable at character creation only for 8th-generation-or-lower characters, elder Status expectations, and 2 starting Beast/Path Traits (rather than the standard 1). The book's worked sample-character example (Alexander/Michael) and the in-fiction "Just Visiting" cautionary tale were excluded as narrative fluff per established convention. The "Only Rules That Matter" player-conduct/safety rules (Its Only a Game, no physical contact, no running/climbing, no weapons, no drugs/drinking, be mindful of non-players, the Golden Rule) were checked against the rest of the pack and found to have no existing equivalent entry in this repo (searched for "Golden Rule," "no weapons," "no drugs," etc. across `packs/`); however, since they are boilerplate LARP safety rules already covered by the core Laws of the Night Revised rulebook (outside this pack's scope) rather than an elder-specific mechanic, they were not duplicated into this file, which focuses solely on the elder-chargen deltas.
