=== CHAPTER ONE: AGREED UPON LIES ===
[Entirely narrative clan history/mythology, framed as annotated field notes by Gwendolyn Brand and Dr. Lawrence Mayhew (the "Grave Robbers," see Chapter Three) after visiting India post-Week of Nightmares. Excluded from compendium per established policy (no mechanics), summarized below for context only.]

LEXICON (terms used throughout book): Alexandrites (Egypt-descended Ravnos lineage), asuratizayya ("countless demons" — the Ravnos' name for Eastern/Kuei-jin vampires), Bashirites (apocalyptic-Christian Ravnos lineage, mostly joined Sabbat), Brahman (jati with "second sight"/prophecy, keepers of Path of Paradox), Chandalas (outcast/impure jati, forbidden to Embrace), jati (Hindu word for caste; Ravnos lineage), Karavalanisha Vrana ("Wounds of the Night's Sword" — the Ravnos clan's epic origin poem, largely fable), Kshatriyas (warrior jati), kumpaniya (a traveling Rroma family/company), mayaparisatya (illusion+truth; refers to the true/Eastern Path of Paradox, or to Chimerstry itself), Phaedymites (vanished visionary Ravnos branch), Phuri Dae (Brahman Ravnos who traveled west with the Rroma), shilmulo (a vampire, in Ravnos usage), siddhi (fallen angelic beings who became the asuratizayya), Sudras (Ravnos ghouls), svadharma (one's individual purpose), Vaisyas (merchant-class jati), Zapathasura (the Ravnos progenitor/Antediluvian, "Accursed Monster").

Origin myth: the siddhi (angelic guardians) betrayed their charge to protect humanity and became the asuratizayya (demons/Eastern Kindred). The gods resurrected a wronged mortal as Zapathasura to hunt them, granting him Animalism, Fortitude, and Chimerstry, and the ability to Embrace childer (unlike the asuratizayya). Zapathasura's first five childer: the Black Mother (prophetess, ancestor of the Brahman/Phuri Dae), the Rakshasa (deceiver, possibly only a ghoul, ancestor of African lineages), Chandraputra the General (ancestor of the Kshatriyas, architect of the Path of Paradox after the clan's near-collapse), Ravana (made an infernal pact and vanished), and Ramessu (ancestor of the Vaisyas, later founded the Egypt-based Alexandrites). A separate "Gangrel Heresy" text describes the gods' second creation, Ennoia (the Gangrel Antediluvian), cursed to bestial form after rejecting her charge — explaining the ancient Ravnos/Gangrel enmity. The "Enoch" fragment ties Ravnos origins to Caine's childe Irad and a childe named Dracian, cursed after a Third Generation revolt. The Mohenjo-Daro/Harappa war describes a legendary conflict between Ravnos-ruled Harappa and asuratizayya-ruled Mohenjo-Daro, ending in mutual annihilation. Following this, Zapathasura's childer scattered (the Diaspora); Chandraputra reorganized the clan around mayaparisatya doctrine.

Roman era: the "Sybarites" (Black Mother's decadent descendants) founded the corrupted, hedonistic Roman "Path of Paradox" (self-indulgence excused as spiritual necessity; requires the destruction of powerful vampires via diablerie to further Gehenna). The Bashirites (founded by a Ravnos named Bashir) merged this Path with apocalyptic Christianity. Medieval period: Rroma migration into Europe brought Chandalas-descended "Rroma Ravnos" who saw themselves as purer-blooded than western Ravnos, leading to feuds and conversions; the "Treatment" tradition emerged (Ravnos retaliating against princes who exile them by unleashing a coordinated wave of crime upon a city). The Inquisition hit Ravnos alongside all Kindred. The Camarilla's founders excluded Ravnos (along with Setites, Assamites, Giovanni) from full status. Age of Exploration/Renaissance: Ravnos spread to Africa/Americas, engaged in piracy, highway robbery, and (some Sybarites) the slave trade. Relatively few Ravnos joined the Sabbat at its founding, mostly ex-Sybarites. Industrial Revolution: Rroma Ravnos began Embracing non-Rroma ("giorgio") in numbers. WWI barely affected the clan; WWII's Porrajmos (the Rroma Holocaust) devastated the Rroma-traveling Ravnos, killing more Ravnos (proportionally) than any other clan. Modern era: post-independence India saw renewed Ravnos/asuratizayya war; the 1984 Bhopal disaster resulted from a botched Kshatriyas mass-Embrace/sabotage operation against a powerful asuratizayya, killing thousands of mortals. In the 1990s, ancient Indian elders awakened and forcibly converted most Western Ravnos from the corrupted Sybarite "Path of Paradox" to the true mayaparisatya.

THE WEEK OF NIGHTMARES: Zapathasura's final battle and destruction (in Bangladesh, apparently via nuclear detonation disguised as a typhoon) triggered a clan-wide psychic catastrophe: Ravnos Disciplines ran wild, and nearly all Ravnos suffered days of blood-crazed rage and diablerie-hunger, destroying each other or being destroyed by other clans/hunters who witnessed the frenzy. Only a small fraction of the clan (perhaps ~100, mostly younger Ravnos who were isolated from other Ravnos at the time) survived; virtually all Indian elders and most Rroma-traveling Ravnos perished. Note for Storytellers: if the Week of Nightmares hasn't occurred in a chronicle, simply ignore all references to it.

=== CHAPTER TWO: ON THE ART OF BEING RAVNOS ===

THE RAVNOS EMBRACE: Indian/Path of Paradox Ravnos treat the Embrace as a formal metaphysical duty requiring sire and eldest-Ravnos-in-city permission; Western Ravnos are informal. Indian Kshatriyas sometimes practice Sabbat-style "mass Embrace" as a weapon against the asuratizayya, using new childer as cannon fodder.

JATI AND BLOODLINES (mechanical note): BRAHMAN Ravnos replace Fortitude with Auspex as a clan Discipline, and are otherwise identical to other Ravnos. KSHATRIYAS are the traditional warrior jati (standard Ravnos disciplines). VAISYAS handle mortal-society interaction/cover-ups (standard Ravnos disciplines). CHANDALAS is the lowest jati (outcasts/Caitiff, forbidden to Embrace; standard disciplines). SUDRAS are not a true jati — the term denotes Ravnos ghouls.

THE WESTERN SHILMULO vs. THE RROMA SHILMULO: Western (non-Rroma) Ravnos form loose, informal coteries with little regard for lineage; Rroma Ravnos traveled tightly bound to Rroma mortal families and suffered catastrophic losses (near-total annihilation) during the Week of Nightmares due to their close proximity to one another.

RAVNOS AROUND THE WORLD (named canon NPCs / lore, "Ravnos of Note" per region):
- INDIA: formerly the largest Kindred power base in Asia; now largely destroyed, elder havens abandoned.
- AFRICA: a small lineage in eastern Africa claims descent from Anansi, the trickster spider god; eldest surviving member calls himself Anansi and works to undermine European Kindred influence.
- ASIA: unconfirmed rumors place a Ravnos elder known as the Flesh-Eater in the Green Courts of the Kuei-jin.
- AUSTRALIA: a small jati, the Seven Phoenix Triad, operates through mortal proxies in Hong Kong but is based in Darwin.
- EUROPE: Durga Syn survives (full entry below); some speculate the Week of Nightmares' curse never touched her, possibly linked to Russia's supernatural isolation.
- MIDDLE EAST: an ancient named Amaravati recently awakened from torpor and led the clan's conversion to true Paradox; reputed to have close ties with the Assamites; vanished during the Week of Nightmares, presumed destroyed.
- NORTH AMERICA: see the Grave Robbers coterie, Chapter Three.
- SOUTH AMERICA: Rafael Schliemann, a Rio de Janeiro-based Ravnos, runs a thriving slave-trade/procurement network stretching across the Pacific and Atlantic; provides herds to select clientele including several Ventrue.

BORN CRIMINALS: THE RAVNOS WEAKNESS (mechanics): Every Ravnos suffers an internal compulsion toward a specific personalized crime, rooted in their mortal life (an indulged temptation, a despised vice turned inward, or something wholly new to their vampiric existence). System: when presented with the opportunity to commit her specific crime, the player must make a Self-Control/Instinct roll (difficulty 6) to resist giving in; MET equivalent: a Static Willpower Challenge (difficulty 6 Traits). The weakness is meant to be personalized per character rather than generic kleptomania. Rroma-descended Ravnos sometimes claim a broader compulsion to break the mores of whatever society they're in, rather than one specific crime, though this is disputed as self-serving rationalization.

THE TREATMENT (concept, narrative/no new mechanics): a coordinated Ravnos retaliation tradition against princes who exile Ravnos unfairly — surviving/travelling Ravnos converge on the offending city and indulge in crime/vice/depredation for weeks or months until the prince relents. Methods described include maintaining a communication network among traveling Ravnos, scouting weak points in a target city via maps/newspapers, and leaving town immediately once the Treatment has begun.

THE LUPINE SURVIVAL GUIDE (Y2K EDITION) — anonymous survival advice for traveling Ravnos to avoid Garou attacks (travel in groups, carry mace/pepper spray, silver-plated tools, road flares, fire extinguishers, keep trips short, scout routes via animal Discipline use, avoid back roads/small towns). Narrative flavor, no new mechanics; notes rumors of a Lupine tribe called "Striders" claiming Ravnos kinship are false.

=== DISCIPLINES ===

ANIMALISM (flavor/no new powers): emphasized as the Ravnos' most underrated Discipline; a Ravnos using Animalism on a target can, per the source text, perceive a limited sense of that target's Beast while the Discipline is active. Optional rule: "Drawing Out the Beast" may be used by a Ravnos to offload the compulsion of the clan weakness onto a victim (Storyteller discretion; carries the standard risks/consequences of that power per core rules, and successfully-used vessels are typically destroyed afterward by the Ravnos to prevent discovery).

FORTITUDE (flavor only, no new powers).

CHIMERSTRY — extensive designer's-note section clarifying Chimerstry is not Obfuscate (affects cameras/sensors, not human perception-filling), governs disbelief (established disbelief doesn't erase existing illusions, only prevents new ones from convincing), area of effect (Storyteller's discretion), interacts with Auspex the same way Obfuscate does (higher Auspex automatically defeats the illusion), and is not "unvampiric." Full elder/master/ascendant/methuselah tier powers, each given full System/MET System text:

FALSE RESONANCE (Chimerstry 6, Elder): Overlays false auras/thoughts onto Chimerstry illusions, defeating Auspex/Dementation reading attempts of 5 or fewer successes, and leaving false emotional resonance traces for Spirit's Touch. System: applies automatically to any Chimerstry use; if an Auspex/Eyes of Chaos roll gets ≤5 successes, it detects only mundane matching thoughts/simple-colored auras. MET: Elder Discipline; contested Mental Challenge (Ravnos' Mental Traits) against any Aura Perception/Telepathy attempt — if the Ravnos wins, false simple information is given; deeper delving reveals nothing beyond surface thoughts.

FATUUS MASTERY (Chimerstry 6, Elder): Removes Blood/Willpower cost for using Ignis Fatuus, Fata Morgana, and Apparition (the first three Chimerstry levels), and lets the Ravnos direct multiple illusions without concentration. System: no Blood/Willpower cost for the first three Chimerstry levels; can direct a number of illusions equal to Intelligence without concentrating; illusion can persist within Willpower score in miles. MET: Elder Discipline; no Trait cost for Basic/first Intermediate Chimerstry; illusions can act complexly without concentration and persist until sunrise unless made permanent with blood.

SHARED NIGHTMARE (Chimerstry 6, Elder): An expansion of Horrid Reality that can afflict multiple targets in a single use. System: spend 1 blood point per target + 2 Willpower; roll Manipulation + Subterfuge once, compare against each target's Perception + Self-Control individually. MET: Elder Discipline; spend 1 Willpower Trait per target (max 5) plus 1 Social Trait bid against all targets simultaneously (compared individually, not a group challenge); losers suffer Horrid Reality's effects.

FAR FATUUS (Chimerstry 7, Master): Lets a Ravnos project illusions to any location she can see, visualize, has visited, or has a photo/description of. System: roll Perception + Subterfuge; difficulty scales with familiarity (6 if as familiar as one's own haven or currently viewed via Clairvoyance/Psychic Projection; 7 if visited 3+ times; 8 if visited once/viewed via live camera feed; 9 if only described in detail; 10 if never visited, only seen on TV/photo). Once successful, any other Chimerstry power may be used at that location. MET: Master Discipline; spend 1 Willpower Trait, win a Static Mental Challenge (difficulty 10 Traits) — waived if location is in the character's line of sight via telescope or Auspex.

SUSPENSION OF DISBELIEF (Chimerstry 7, Master): Makes onlookers believe any Chimerstry illusion is real regardless of how outlandish, or (used inversely) makes something real seem unbelievable/dismissed as a trick. System: roll Manipulation + Subterfuge (difficulty 7); successes determine how many witnesses are affected (1=5 people, 2=10, 3=25, 4=50, 5=everyone who can see it). MET: Master Discipline; spend 3 Social Traits; witnesses with reason to disbelieve must win a Static Social Challenge (difficulty = Ravnos' current Social Traits) or believe it implicitly.

OCCLUSION (Chimerstry 8, Ascendant): Scrambles a target's (or crowd's) senses into disorienting synesthesia, rendering them unable to meaningfully function. System: single target — spend 1 Willpower, roll Manipulation + Intimidation (difficulty = victim's Willpower); crowd — difficulty 7, affects everyone in line of sight, -1 Perception per success (Perception 0 = totally incapacitated). Duration vs. single target: 1 success=1 week, 2=1 month, 3=6 months, 4=1 year, 5=permanent; vs. crowd, lasts until sunrise. MET: Ascendant Discipline; spend 1 Willpower Trait, win a Static Social Challenge (difficulty 12 Traits), then spend 1 Social Trait per target; affected targets must spend 1 Willpower Trait to act at all, must bid 2 Traits in all challenges, and automatically lose all ties. Lasts until sunrise or the Ravnos releases the target.

MAYAPARISATYA (Chimerstry 9, Methuselah): The Path of Paradox's namesake power; lets the Ravnos directly alter or destroy real objects/creatures for the scene's duration. System: spend 10 blood points, roll Manipulation + Subterfuge (difficulty 6 for inanimate objects, victim's Willpower for conscious targets); successes determine severity (1=render harmless/create smoke, 2=transmute into another object, 3=render insubstantial, 4=drastic transmutation, 5=locally suspend physical laws, 6+=permanently delete matter). Against conscious targets, alternately inflicts 2 unsoakable aggravated damage per success, or -1 Strength/Stamina dot per success (temporary). MET: Methuselah Discipline; spend 10 Blood Traits, win a Static Mental Challenge (difficulty 6 Traits) vs. objects or a Mental Challenge vs. conscious targets; spend 1 Mental Trait per aspect changed or per level of unreducible aggravated damage inflicted (short of Methuselah-level Fortitude). Effects on inanimate objects may apply to everything of that type within line of sight at no extra cost.

NEW DERANGEMENT — DISSOCIATIVE PERCEPTIONS SYNDROME: Since the Week of Nightmares, heavy Chimerstry use has begun corroding Ravnos' own grip on reality. After a Ravnos completely depletes his Willpower (to zero temporary Willpower) creating an illusion, he suffers The Haunting (per Vampire: The Masquerade Revised, p.155, Dementation). With continued Chimerstry overuse, this progresses to full-sensory hallucinations of escalating severity and danger (mistaking friend for foe, perceiving a busy street as empty, etc.); the character can spend 1 Willpower point to negate hallucinations for a scene if he recognizes them as such.

=== COMBINATION DISCIPLINES ===

SYMPATHETIC AGONY (Chimerstry + Fortitude): Developed by the Kshatriyas caste to transfer wound-pain (and effectively the wound itself, for a time) onto an attacker. System: spend 1 blood point, roll Manipulation + Intimidation (difficulty = victim's Perception + Self-Control); each success lets the Ravnos ignore one health level of damage and inflict that level's pain on the attacker instead (the Ravnos still has the wound, just doesn't feel it) for the scene. Requires Basic Chimerstry + Intermediate Fortitude; costs 10 XP to learn (text gives both "20 experience points" for the dot-system version and "10 Experience Traits" for MET — MET is the canonical live-play value per established convention in this book). MET: spend 1 Blood Trait, win a Social Challenge (Intimidation retest) vs. target; spend 1 Social Trait per level of damage transferred; Endurance does not block this transferred pain.

HEART'S DESIRE (Auspex + Chimerstry): Reveals and manifests an illusory physical token of a target's greatest desire, useful for manipulation. System: spend 1 blood point + 1 Willpower, roll Intelligence + Subterfuge (difficulty = target's Willpower), 3 successes needed; once discovered, the token can be recreated at will for the same cost unless the target's desire changes. Requires Intermediate Auspex + Basic Chimerstry; costs 20 XP (dot system) / 10 Experience Traits (MET). MET: spend 1 Blood Trait + 1 Willpower Trait, win a Mental Challenge vs. target to discover the desire; thereafter recreate the illusion at will using standard Fata Morgana.

EASTERN MASK (Animalism + Chimerstry): Lets a Ravnos operating in Kuei-jin (Cathayan) territory disguise her supernatural nature as that of a yin-aspected Kuei-jin rather than Kindred, to any power/sense that would otherwise detect her true nature. Does not grant any Eastern Discipline or Dharma access. System: spend 1 Willpower + 1 blood point, roll Manipulation + Subterfuge (difficulty 6); successes = number an onlooker's detection power must overcome; lasts one night. Requires Intermediate Animalism + Chimerstry; costs 15 XP (dot system) / 7 Experience Traits (MET). MET: spend 1 Willpower Trait + 1 Blood Trait to invoke until sunrise; during that time the Ravnos registers via supernatural senses as a yin-aspected Cathayan, not as Kindred.

RAVNOS OF NOTE (named canon NPCs — lore-only entries per established Cardinal Monçada/Nosferatu Venerable precedent, to be built as lore JournalEntry pages even without full stat blocks):
- DURGA SYN: ancient pre-Christian nature priestess, cursed into a withered crone by the Nosferatu Methuselah Baba Yaga for refusing the Embrace, then Embraced against her will by rival Ravnos to spite Baba Yaga. Warred against Baba Yaga for centuries, advised Vlad the Impaler in his quest for Golconda, developed rituals to temporarily banish her curse. Staked by her own childer during the Week of Nightmares to protect her from Zapathasura's curse; emerged to find Baba Yaga destroyed and her ancient curse fading.
